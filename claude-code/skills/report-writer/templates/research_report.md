# Research Report

## Result

What was established, within which conditions, and why it is useful.

## Evidence

The experiment, baseline, metric, uncertainty, and artifact pointers needed to assess and reproduce this result. Identify synthetic, simulated, or measured evidence as applicable.

## Interpretation And Next Action

Explain the supported interpretation and material limitations. For a weak or negative result, distinguish known causes from hypotheses and propose remedies with discriminating checks. State the next action that the evidence warrants.

Omit sections that add no information; use existing logs and configuration instead of repeating them.
