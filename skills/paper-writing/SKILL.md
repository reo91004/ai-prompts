# Paper Writing

Make the work read as one scientific paper, with a clear purpose and the strongest honest supported contribution. Use the language, venue template, and author-approved reference manuscript requested for the project. Research reports, execution logs, and handovers use `report-writer`.

Read [manuscript rules](references/manuscript_rules.md) when drafting or substantively revising a manuscript. For claim selection or interpretation, use the relevant [shared research principles](../research-domain-router/references/research_principles.md). Preserve numerical values, units, citations, sampling units, important conditions, and uncertainty while removing internal history and unnecessary defensive prose.

Use the installed `humanizer` skill for the prose-editing pass, especially before delivering a manuscript. Keep equations, scientific terminology, claims, citations, code, and author-owned final-edit regions intact. Humanizer's stylistic advice does not authorize a factual change or a stronger claim. Read its instructions when performing that pass; do not merely assert that it ran. A small edit needs only the relevant local check and prose pass, not a fresh research review.
